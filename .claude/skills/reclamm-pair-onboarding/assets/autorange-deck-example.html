Parameters

How does it work?

How wide is the liquidity range?
The Price Ratio (PR) defines the width - the ratio between the highest and lowest price the pool actively covers.
A higher PR means a wider range: fewer shifts, but less concentrated liquidity.
When does the range start shifting?
The Margin is a buffer zone at each edge of the range - once price enters it, the pool begins re-centering.
A tighter margin means fewer shifts, lower arb cost per adjustment, but less tolerance for fast moves.
How fast does it shift?
The Shift Speed is proportional to how off-center the pool is - faster when lagging, gentler when nearly aligned.
No oracle or keeper needed. Re-centering is continuous and fully on-chain.

Methodology

How do we calibrate this pool?

The three parameters interact in non-obvious ways - wrong combinations can erode LP performance.

The QuantAMM simulator replays every configuration against real market data, giving a calibrated, defensible recommendation.

Arb flow, organic swap volume, and competitor pool liquidity are all modelled from on-chain data.

Arb Simulation

Price history replayed. Every arbitrage trade is computed from real price data with live gas costs.

Organic Volume Model

A calibrated Michaelis-Menten routing model separates genuine swap-fee income from arb costs. Organic flow is anchored to the token's real traded volume and scales with how much of the pair's liquidity this pool actually holds.

3
Full Sensitivity Sweep

1,000s of scenarios tested - 165 parameter combos across 5 rolling 2-month windows. Ranked by a blended POL score (HODL-return + fee yield), so the optimum reflects every market regime, not a best-case window.

Automated grid search

Every combination of parameters is tested by the simulator across 5 rolling 2-month windows over INV's last 6 months.

Each cell is the median return vs HODL across the windows — greener is less divergence loss. The surface tilts green toward the wider right edge: on INV's one-way drawdown, looser ranges track HODL more closely.

The top candidates are then reviewed manually - looking at consistency across regimes, robustness to INV's sharp drawdowns, and alignment with Inverse's risk appetite - before a final recommendation is made.

For protocol-owned liquidity we balanced HODL performance and fee / volume generation - keeping the basket intact while making INV/DOLA liquidity productive.
